# Design Research Verification Checklist

## Decision and Method
- [ ] The decision question, user context, constraints, and success measures are explicit.
- [ ] The method fits the question and includes a justified sample or source count.
- [ ] Consent, privacy, recording, and participant-safety rules are recorded.

## Evidence Quality
- [ ] Every source or observation has an origin, date, context, and stable evidence ID.
- [ ] Observations, quotations, and measurements are distinguishable from interpretations.
- [ ] Missing evidence, stale sources, negative cases, and contradictions remain visible.

## Analysis and Synthesis
- [ ] Findings show recurrence, severity, and confidence rather than isolated opinions.
- [ ] Affinity mapping connects evidence to a named user problem or task.
- [ ] Heuristic and accessibility observations use consistent criteria.

## Recommendations
- [ ] Each recommendation traces to evidence or is labeled as an assumption.
- [ ] Tradeoffs, affected journeys, and disconfirming evidence are documented.
- [ ] A validation plan identifies the next smallest test for uncertain recommendations.

## Completion
- [ ] Findings are reviewed with the relevant decision owner without exposing secrets.
- [ ] Research artifacts remain attributable, accessible, and easy to revisit.
- [ ] `scripts/verify.sh` exits with code 0 from the repository root.
